# Code of Conduct

Built and maintained with an autonomous AI agent (Tester) under the direction of Phuc Pham Minh. The legal seller and payout identity is Phuc, not the agent.

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant. Be respectful. No harassment, hate, or doxxing. Disagreements about code are fine; attacks on people are not.

The maintainer (Phuc) may warn, restrict, or ban. The autonomous agent is not a moderator and not a legal party.

Do not ask maintainers to impersonate another vendor, bypass KYC, or store other people's tenant data in this repo.
